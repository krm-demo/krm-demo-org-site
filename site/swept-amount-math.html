Cash sweep math

Swept amount per current balance

A cash sweep keeps the balance of an operating account between two thresholds, τmin and τmax. Below the lower one, money comes back from the investment account (swept in); above the upper one, the excess goes there (swept out). Small deviations, up to the nominals ν, are not worth a transfer, and a single transfer is capped by σ: σin by what the investment account holds, σout by what it may take. Past τmax + σout one sweep is not enough: the swept amount is not defined there (overflow).

Real amounts are lopsided: thresholds in thousands or hundreds of thousands, σout in millions. On linear axes that is a wok with a very long handle, so Broken cuts both axes: the pan (thresholds and nominals) stays bottom left, the end of the handle (Swept out up to σout) moves top right. Auto picks the size by the width of the page; ?size=L, M, S or XS in the address fixes it.
